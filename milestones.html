LAB MILESTONES

A timeline tracking our evolution from an experimental spatial computing workgroup to a comprehensive research facility powering 6 specialized multidisciplinary divisions.

★ RECENT ACHIEVEMENTS
◆ LAB HISTORY & TRAJECTORY
2026
CURRENT HORIZON

Six Specialized Research Divisions Operational

Formal launch of the 6 unified research divisions spanning Engineering Simulations, Spatial Career Placements, Surgical Healthcare VR, Cultural Heritage Digital Twins, Interactive Entertainment, and Smart Infrastructure.

2025
INNOVATION AWARD

National Hackathon Gold in Spatial Computing

Student research teams secured 1st prize at national XR innovation hackathons for real-time CAD model disassembly inside standalone Quest headsets using custom gesture recognizers.

2024
INFRASTRUCTURE UPGRADE

Deployment of High-End Spatial Graphics Rigs

Equipped the lab with dedicated NVIDIA RTX workstations, 6DoF tracking arrays, and photogrammetry scanning rigs to enable full photorealistic digital twin authoring in Unreal Engine 5.

2023
ACADEMIC FOUNDATION

Founding of GCET AR/VR Research Lab

Established under department leadership with an initial cohort of passionate student engineers to bridge theoretical computer graphics with real-world extended reality systems.
